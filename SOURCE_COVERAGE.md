# Source Coverage

The configuration uses public employer career systems and official career pages. It does not log in to or scrape LinkedIn.

| Employer | Source method | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Audi F1 / Sauber | Personio public feed | Professional openings |
| Audi Formula Racing students | Official Personio career page | Only matching finance/experience titles are retained |
| Ford Racing | Ford Oracle Recruiting feed | Source-specific filter requires Ford Racing, Ford Performance, motorsport, or motorsports; Ford corporate finance and Ford Credit are removed |
| McLaren Racing | Recruitee public feed | McLaren Racing career account |
| Aston Martin F1 | Pinpoint public feed | Official F1 team board |
| Williams Racing | Official vacancy page | Direct job links |
| Cadillac F1 | Official Cadillac F1 career pages | Direct job links |
| Mercedes-AMG PETRONAS F1 | Official vacancy page | Direct vacancy links |
| Red Bull Racing & Technology | Official careers page | Page-based source; verify workflow logs if it reports zero jobs |
| Andretti Global | ADP Workforce Now public recruiting feed | Official career center |
| Visa Cash App Racing Bulls | Official VCARB/Red Bull career pages | Direct job links |
| Alpine Racing | Workday public recruiting feed | Alpine Racing site only |
| Haas F1 Team | BambooHR public feed | Official F1 team board |
| Ferrari | Official Ferrari jobs page | Direct job links |

## Intentionally removed

- Ford Global Finance
- Ford Credit Services
